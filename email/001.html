The record went to Paris, by phone, to Asia

Plus, why a guaranteed lot is a financing deal, and a Ferrari its buyer cannot take home.

1. From the editor's desk

The most expensive lot of the past month had no safety net. A Monet made €50 million in Paris with no guarantee, after six bidders fought over it for about 45 minutes. Eight days earlier in London, all 41 lots in the Peter Simon collection had a guaranteed price before the auctioneer started. When a guaranteed lot sells near its low estimate, you are reading the backer's price, not the market's.

2. The number

17% to 27%. The fee the auction house added on top of the winning bid, on the 11 lots this month where both figures were published. The rate falls as the price rises: 17% on the $42 million Kandinsky, 27% on a $5 million Rothko. Estimates leave the fee out and headline prices include it, so check which one you are reading.

3. Three sales

Claude Monet, Iris
€50M ($56.02M) · Sotheby's Paris, 22 Oct 2026
The most expensive artwork ever sold at auction in France, with no guarantee and six bidders over 45 minutes.

F.P. Journe, Chronomètre à Résonance 'Régence', platinum
CHF 8.68M ($10.6M) · Phillips Geneva, 7 Nov 2026
A watch by a living maker at CHF 8.7m against an estimate of 'in excess of CHF 1m', in what Phillips calls the first live watch auction to pass $100m.

Ferrari, 499P Modificata
CHF 8.22M ($10.04M) · Christie's Geneva, 8 Nov 2026
A barely driven, track-only car the buyer cannot take home: it stays at Ferrari in Maranello until December 2028 and runs through a factory programme.

4. Who bought

Two of 34 buyers are on the record.

Beaumont Nathan: Liubov Popova, Painterly Architectonic, £7.64M ($10.14M). Art advisers in London and New York; reported by Apollo, citing the Baer Faxt newsletter. Whether the firm bought for a client is not stated.

Shawn Mehta: F.P. Journe, Chronomètre à Résonance 'Pre-Production' No. 022/99R, platinum, CHF 4.94M ($6.03M). Head of F.P. Journe's London boutique; reported by Hodinkee, which says he later told its author he bid for a friend and not for the brand.

The other 32 are anonymous. Art Moves does not print guesses about private people.

5. One term

Guarantee. A promise made before the sale, by the house or an outside investor, to buy the lot at a set price if nobody bids higher. The seller gets certainty and the backer gets a fee, or the work. Treat it like an underwritten offering.
